Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

1. Collected Data

NamazVaktim Pro uses the following data:

Location Data: Your device's location information is used to calculate prayer times and determine the Qibla direction. Precise location (GPS) is processed only on your device; only approximate, city/district-level location is sent to prayer-times servers to fetch official prayer times.

Advertising ID: The Google AdMob SDK uses your device's advertising ID to serve ads in the app. This is standard operation of Google's advertising services.

2. Use of Data

The data used by NamazVaktim Pro is used only for the following purposes:

  • Calculating prayer times according to your location
  • Determining the Qibla direction
  • Setting prayer time reminders
  • Playing adhan audio at prayer times
  • Displaying ads via Google AdMob

3. Data Sharing

NamazVaktim Pro does not sell or rent your personal data. However, the following sharing is required for the app to function:

  • Google AdMob: Your advertising ID is shared with Google to display ads. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.
  • Prayer Times Servers: City/district-level location is sent to prayer-times APIs to fetch official prayer times. Your precise GPS coordinates are never sent.

4. Data Retention

Location data is used only to calculate the current prayer times and is not permanently stored on your device.

Your in-app preferences, such as selected city and dhikr counts, are stored locally on your device only.

5. Security

The data used by NamazVaktim Pro is processed securely on your device. Data sent over the internet (approximate location for prayer times, advertising ID for ads) contains no personally identifying information and is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

6. Children's Privacy

NamazVaktim Pro is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
